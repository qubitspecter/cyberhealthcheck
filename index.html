Cyber Health Check

Why does this page exist?

Cyber security looks like an industry that is failing. Major breaches now happen daily. The smaller ones land closer to home: it is hard to meet friends without one of them knowing someone directly affected by a hack since the last catch up.

Every MSP says they are cyber security experts. Every business says it handles your data securely. Are they lying? Do they not know any better? Or are they doing everything right, and this is as good as it gets?

This service exists to get past the marketing. We look at how businesses, big and small, actually use security, and we look for real answers as objectively as we can. There is no sponsorship. We are a few frustrated laypeople with research backgrounds, tired of being told our records have been stolen yet again.
