FACILITY BENCHMARKS

Core Workstation & Environmental Standards

Reference parameters governing night-cycle terminal operations, surface sanitization, and ergonomic alignment.

MODULE 01 50–70 CM

Optical & Postural Ergonomics

Primary display monitors must sit at arm's length with the top bezel aligned at or slightly below horizontal eye level to eliminate cervical flexion strain during extended terminal monitoring.

  • Elbow Angle: 90°–105° neutral forearm plane
  • Luminance Ratio: 3:1 max task-to-ambient contrast
MODULE 02 70% IPA

Peripheral Surface Hygiene

High-contact input surfaces—including mechanical keycaps, optical mice, and badge readers—require scheduled disinfection using lint-free wipes dampened with 70% isopropyl alcohol.

  • Dwell Time: Minimum 30 seconds wet contact
  • Cycle Interval: Between every shift handover
